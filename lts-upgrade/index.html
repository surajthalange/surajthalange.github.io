Service

Fixed-Price LTS Upgrade

Upgrades to the 5.3 long-term support release for the Moodle™ platform, at a price fixed before work starts.

Email surajthalange18@gmail.com → Start with a health check

I'm Suraj Thalange, an independent LMS developer and technical project manager. I've upgraded sites across versions 3.7 to 5.2, built a 250,000-user, 1,500-course platform single-handed for a government training body, and I write and maintain open-source plugins and a theme. Alongside LMS work, I build custom software in Laravel and PHP.

Why 5.3 LTS: 4.5 LTS loses security support on 4 October 2027. 5.3 LTS is supported until October 2029, so one upgrade now covers you for the next few years.

What's included

  • Upgrade on a staging copy first, never straight on your live site
  • Plugin and theme compatibility: update, patch or replace, as agreed in the quote
  • Fixes to your custom code where needed
  • Your team tests on staging and signs off
  • Live upgrade in an agreed maintenance window, usually a weekend
  • Rollback plan and fresh backup before the live upgrade
  • Upgrade notes: what changed, what to tell your users
  • 14 days of fixes for any issue caused by the upgrade

Optional add-ons

  • AI features setup: connecting an AI provider, setting cost limits and turning on the AI tools your teachers will actually use
  • Theme refresh
  • Move to new hosting

Price

Fixed price after the health check. Typically USD 900 to 2,500, depending on the number of custom plugins and how far behind your site is.
50% to start, 50% after the live upgrade.

What's not included

Rewriting plugins whose authors have abandoned them (quoted separately if needed), content changes, hosting fees, and anything not listed in the agreed quote.
