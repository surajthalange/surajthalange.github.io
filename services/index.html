LMS implementation

A Moodle platform set up on your cloud account and configured for how your organisation actually enrols, trains and reports. Suited to corporate compliance training, professional certification and course providers moving off a hosted product.

  • Moodle 5.x on AWS or the cloud you already use, with staging and production, backups and a restore runbook
  • Roles and permissions narrowed to what each team needs, documented capability by capability
  • Enrolment, completion, certificates and notifications configured, not customised, wherever core does the job
  • Mail, SSO and HR or CRM integrations where you need them

Example: compliance LMS for a fintech company

You receive

  • A working platform your team has clicked through on staging
  • Operations runbook and admin guide
  • Role and capability register
  • A handover call and 30 days of post-launch fixes

Custom plugin development

Activity modules, report plugins, blocks, local plugins, admin tools and web service integrations, written to Moodle's coding standards with the tests and CI the Moodle Marketplace expects. Suited to teams that need something core does not do and want it to survive the next upgrade.

  • Moodle 4.5 LTS to 5.2, PHP 8.1 to 8.4, PostgreSQL and MariaDB
  • PHPUnit and Behat for the behaviour that matters, run in GitHub Actions on every change
  • Privacy provider, capabilities, language strings, backup and restore, upgrade steps: the whole surface, not just the happy path
  • Marketplace submission if you want the plugin published

Example: mod_onelife, report_transcript

You receive

  • A versioned plugin in a git repository you own
  • CI configured and green
  • README with installation, settings and known limitations
  • Upgrade path for future Moodle releases

Themes and branding

Boost child themes that implement your brand guideline and stay upgrade-safe. Accessibility is built in from the first commit: contrast, keyboard navigation, focus management and screen reader behaviour are tested, not promised.

  • Colour, type, logo use, login page, email templates and front page from your guideline
  • Settings pages so your team can change content without a developer
  • Dark mode, RTL languages and mobile layouts where you need them
  • Behat coverage for every page a user interacts with

Example: theme_atrium

You receive

  • A theme plugin with an override register listing every core template touched
  • Screenshots and a settings guide
  • Accessibility configuration and testing against common WCAG 2.1 AA failures, with written notes. Formal accessibility audit is not included.

Reporting and automation

Reports that match the spreadsheet your compliance or L&D team already files, scheduled exports, reminder and escalation mailers, and clean-up tasks that remove manual admin work.

  • Custom report plugins or Report builder datasources, whichever fits
  • Scheduled tasks with settings pages and logs
  • Numbers checked against known data before you rely on them

You receive

  • Reports in your format, on demand and on a schedule
  • Mailers whose timing and audience are settings, not code
  • Documentation of every rule the numbers follow

Delivery leadership

Owning a platform delivery, or leading the team that is building one. For organisations that have the budget and the people but need someone accountable for the plan, the sequence and the date.

  • Requirements and BRDs, estimation, sprint planning and a milestone plan with acceptance criteria
  • Day-to-day coordination of development, QA, UI/UX and infrastructure
  • UAT run against the criteria agreed up front, with defects triaged with you
  • Rehearsed production deployment with a tested rollback, then post-go-live support
  • Written status and risk to stakeholders at every milestone

How an engagement runs

Fixed scope, short milestones, written handover

  1. Call

    Thirty minutes on what you need and what you already have. If Moodle core covers it, I will say so, and show you on the live demo.

  2. Brief and quote

    A written brief: what is in, what is out, milestones and how we will know each is done. A fixed price per milestone, or a day rate for open-ended work.

  3. Build and review

    Each milestone ends with a walkthrough on staging and a delivery note listing what changed, what to test and what is still open.

  4. Handover

    Runbooks, guides, repository access and a handover call. Post-launch fixes included; support after that on a retainer if you want it.
